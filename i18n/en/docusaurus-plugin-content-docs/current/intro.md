# Welcome to FatNotes

FatNotes is a local-first desktop knowledge workspace. It brings notes, data dashboards, workflow automation, and AI into one professional interface—so knowledge can be captured, connected, analyzed, and used.

## What can you do with FatNotes?

- **Capture knowledge** with Markdown, rich text, and document widgets.
- **Understand data** by connecting databases and creating dashboards.
- **Automate work** with visual workflows for data and repetitive tasks.
- **Retrieve intelligently** with AI grounded in your own knowledge.
- **Extend the workspace** through a flexible plugin architecture.
